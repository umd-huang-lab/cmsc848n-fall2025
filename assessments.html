Grading Rubrics

Reports (Mid & Final)

  • Problem statement: Clear and high clarity
  • Literature survey: Thorough and deep
  • Plans: Clear layout of plans going forward
  • Writing quality: Overall high-quality writing
  • Results (final report only): Sound experimental/empirical results and analysis

Presentations

  • Time management: Stay within the assigned time slot (7-9 minutes presentation + 2-3 minutes Q&A)
  • Slides clarity: No full pages of text; use bullet points and keywords, high-resolution images, consistent fonts and formatting
  • Communication clarity: Speak loudly and confidently with a coherent storytelling flow; be interactive and responsive to the audience
  • Novelty (research projects only): How novel the proposed idea is
  • Comprehensiveness (research & engineering projects): Coverage of models, data, domains, tasks, baselines, and ablation studies where appropriate
